      Install & offline

      This device right now

        📱 iPhone / iPad

        1. Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or an in-app browser).
        2. Wait until the dot next to the title turns green and says “available offline”.
        3. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow up), scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
        4. Open the app from the new icon once while connected to the internet.

        🤖 Android

        1. Open this page in Chrome.
        2. Wait for the green dot (“available offline”).
        3. Tap the ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
        4. Open the app from the new icon once while online.

        ✅ Make sure it works offline

        1. With internet on, open the app from the home-screen icon and wait for the green dot.
        2. Open each game once so everything it needs is saved.
        3. Turn on Airplane mode, close the app fully (swipe it away) and open it again from the icon.
        4. Open a game. If it plays, you're all set.

        After new games are added, open the app with internet and tap ↻ so they're saved too. If the phone hasn't opened the app for a few weeks, it may clear saved files; just open it once online again.
